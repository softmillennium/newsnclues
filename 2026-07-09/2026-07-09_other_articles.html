According to British media reports, U.S. President Donald Trump allegedly already has a speech prepared in which he may publicly address UFOs and the possible existence of extraterrestrial life for the first time. This was stated by British ufologist and filmmaker Mark Christopher Lee. According to him, the American leader is preparing for a new phase of disclosing information related to unidentified flying objects and possible extraterrestrials. Lee claims that the speech has already been written and is just waiting to be delivered. Furthermore, he suggested that the delivery of this speech was allegedly scheduled to coincide with the NATO summit in Turkey on July 8, although it did not take place on that day. "The people I spoke with in Washington told me that a speech on this topic has been prepared and is ready to be delivered. Lee claims that Donald Trump intends to publicly state that these incidents—rumors of which have been “circulating” for years—are linked to flying objects and beings of “extraterrestrial origin.” Li also said that he received this information specifically from American businessman and billionaire Robert Bigelow, known for his work in real estate, the hotel industry, and space technology. The ufologist also mentioned American television producer David Broadwell, who helped create the series *Beyond Skinwalker Ranch*, dedicated to investigating paranormal phenomena and UFO-related events. Broadwell confirmed this information to reporters and said that his sources in Washington “were all talking about this speech.” Although the exact content of this speech—the existence of which has not been confirmed—is unknown, the publication notes that Mark Christopher Lee's previous predictions regarding UFOs have come true. In particular, he had stated long before this that the release of information regarding UFOs would begin this year, and that starting in May, the Pentagon would begin releasing materials on UFOs. Trump stated that he does not consider the NATO summit in Turkey to be his last. Earlier, Trump spoke of his good relations with Zelenskyy. U.S. President Donald Trump said he is ready to visit Ukraine “at the appropriate time,” but believes it would be best to do so after the end of the Russian-Ukrainian war. Among the main topics of discussion were strengthening Ukraine's air defense, supplying missiles for the Patriot systems, as well as continued military support for Ukraine and exploring ways to achieve peace. Donald Trump made these remarks during a meeting with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy on the sidelines of the NATO summit.
