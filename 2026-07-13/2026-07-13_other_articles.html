How I embraced my spiritual side on a Psychic Sisters retreat in the Caribbean
Source: {'href': 'https://www.womenshealthmag.com', 'title': "Women's Health"}    Published: 2026-07-13 08:12:32

Tarot, crystals and learning to trust your intuition at StolenTime resort in St Lucia Soft, ethereal music is playing in the background, and I'm sat in a circle with four other women I had only met the day before. Not quite what I had in mind when I set off for a few days on the beautiful Caribbean island of St. Lucia. The group are all here with one thing in common: to tap into our own intuition and disconnect from the stresses of everyday life. It's designed as part beach break, part spiritual deep dive - and I hope to leave with more than just a tan. Although there are plenty of high-adrenaline water sports on offer if that takes your fancy (inflatable donut ride, anyone? There's a no-TV policy in the rooms - I even found myself switching off from the Wi-Fi too, something I would struggle with at home. The main event, though, is the four-day retreat led by Jayne Wallace – infamous clairvoyant whose client list includes A-listers (yes, including Kim Kardashian). She's not remotely what you might expect: less mysterious Mystic Meg, more straight-talking East Londoner. As a group, each day we're encouraged to open ourselves up to the Psychic Sisters' signature spiritual practices and a transformative journey of self-discovery. Sounds deep, but as the certified ‘witchy' one in my friendship group, I'm excited to learn more. Across the four days, Jayne leads us through a curated series of workshops, starting gently with a guided meditation to open our chakras (and the aforementioned tears), helping us to develop and trust our intuition. We then move on to aura drawings - something I knew absolutely nothing about. I begin to see colours appearing and enveloping the shape of my partner. We learn what each colour represents: my partner's energy field shows lots of green (healing energy) and yellow hues (abundance). This is the part I'm most excited for. I've had my cards read before, but learning how to use my own intuition to read them myself is fascinating. Jayne explains that everyone has this ability, and with practice, we can hone in on our gut instincts. Once again, it surprisingly comes naturally to me (a theme on this trip) and I start to wonder if maybe Jayne is right. Practising on each other is one thing; doing it for unsuspecting hotel staff while Wallace casually drops eerily accurate insights is another and we're all in awe of her talents. Even if you don't fully buy into the spiritual framework, there's something powerful about being forced to stop, reflect without your phone buzzing every five seconds. However, this unique experience leaves me with a strong sense of clarity and confidence in my own intuition. In a world where algorithms tell us what to watch, buy and think, there's something refreshing about being encouraged to trust your own gut instead.
